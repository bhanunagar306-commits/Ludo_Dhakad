Ludo Dhakad: Privacy Policy

Publisher: BR Astra Studio · Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

Ludo Dhakad does not collect, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things only on your own device, using your browser or app storage: your chosen theme, sound and music volume, player names and colours you type, an unfinished game (for Continue Game), and your local stats and achievements. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by using the Reset button in the Stats screen or by clearing the app's data in Windows or your browser.

Internet use

The game is delivered as a web app. After the first launch it works offline. Loading or updating the game files uses an internet connection, but no personal data is sent by the game.

Children

The game does not ask for any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
